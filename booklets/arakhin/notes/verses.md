# v_Leviticus_27 1-2
**איש כי יפלא נדר בערכך נפשת.** Rashi reads *yafli* as "states explicitly with his mouth". The Mishnah's opening "הכל" uses the word **איש** to include the *mufla samuch le'ish*, the boy just short of adulthood who understands what he vows (1:1). The Mishnah sets "בני ישראל" against "איש" in the case of the nochri (1:2). **נפשת** is the source for "a thing on which life depends": "the *erech* of my head or my liver" pays the full *erech*, while "the *erech* of my hand" pays nothing (5:2–3). **בערכך** with its doubled *kaf* teaches that the *erech* is of the whole person, not of limbs. Rashi on the Chumash says he does not know what kind of form the doubled *kaf* is. Tosafos Yom Tov on 5:2 defends Rashi against the Re'em's question.

# v_Leviticus_27 3-7
**The fixed amounts.** Rashi: "This *erech* is not a price. Dear or cheap, it is fixed by his years." That is "יש בערכין להקל ולהחמיר" (3:1). Ages are counted from the pledge (4:1), and the sixtieth, twentieth and fifth years count as the bracket below (4:4). "From one month" means there is no *erech* for anyone younger (1:1). After sixty a man drops by more than two thirds (50 to 15) and a woman by only two thirds (30 to 10). Rashi quotes the Gemara (19a): "an old man in the house is a breach in the house; an old woman in the house is a treasure in the house."

# v_Leviticus_27 8
**ואם מך הוא מערכך.** This is the source of *hasagas yad*, ability to pay, which is measured by the **pledger** (הנודר; 4:1). "**והעמידו** לפני הכהן" excludes the goses (1:3). Bartenura and Rambam also use it to require "standing in judgment" before the heirs must pay (5:2). It underlies the question about kohanim in 1:1. **מך הוא** means poor "from beginning to end" for R' Yehudah (4:2). It also means "let *him* keep enough to live": food, clothing, bed, tefillin and tools are left to him (6:3; Rashi). The minimum of one sela comes from 27:25 (2:1).

# v_Leviticus_27 9-13
**Animals.** Consecrating a limb of an animal fit for the altar, for example "its leg is an *olah*", takes effect on the whole animal. It is sold for *olos*, and the money is *chullin* except for the value of that limb (Rashi). An animal unfit for the altar (טמאה, which Rashi explains as blemished) is redeemed at the kohen's valuation. The **owner adds a fifth** (27:13). The same rule for an owner appears with houses, fields and *ma'aser sheni* (Rashi). That is why the owners "open first" and "come first" in the bidding (8:1–3).

# v_Leviticus_27 14-15
**A consecrated house** is redeemed at its assessed value, with the owner's fifth. R' Yehudah learns from it, by the *gezeirah shavah* קדש–קדש, that an unredeemed field does not go to the kohanim free (7:4).

# v_Leviticus_27 16-21
**Sedeh achuzah.** **לפי זרעו**: by its sowing area, not its quality. A *beis kor* of barley costs 50 shekels, whether sandy coastland or the orchards of Sebaste (3:2). Redeemed in the middle of the cycle, it costs "a sela and a pundyon a year" (Rashi; 7:1). There is no redemption "**על פי השנים הנותרת**" with less than two years left (7:1). The owner adds a fifth (27:19; 7:2). **ואם מכר את השדה לאיש אחר**, meaning the gizbar sold it: then at Yovel it goes **to the kohanim**. "Another" excludes the owner's son (7:3). **קדש לה' כשדה החרם**: it is shared among the kohanim of the *mishmar* on duty on Yom Kippur of the Yovel year (Rashi). The dispute over whether the kohanim pay for it is in 7:4. **בצאתו ביבל** is R' Eliezer's source for the "abandoned field" (7:4).

# v_Leviticus_27 22-24
**Sedeh mikneh.** A purchased field would return to its seller at Yovel, so it never goes to the kohanim. "A person cannot consecrate what is not his" (7:5). **מכסת הערכך** means its value. That is the Tanna Kamma's reading against R' Eliezer, who uses the *gezeirah shavah* וחשב–וחשב (3:2). **אשר לא משדה אחזתו** means "a field not fit to become an *achuzah*". That is the basis for R' Yehudah and R' Shimon on the field bought from one's father (7:5). Rashi explains **לאשר קנהו מאתו לאשר לו אחזת הארץ**: the second phrase rules out returning it to the treasurer.

# v_Leviticus_27 25
**וכל ערכך יהיה בשקל הקדש** is the source that no *erech* is less than one shekel (sela) (2:1). **עשרים גרה**: twenty *ma'os*. The Sages later raised it by a sixth, to 24 *ma'os* to the sela (Rashi).

# v_Leviticus_27 26-27
**The bechor** cannot be consecrated as a different *korban* (לא יקדיש). But it can be devoted or consecrated for its *tovas hana'ah*, R' Yishmael's "הקדש עילוי" (8:7). Rashi explains 27:27 as a non-kosher animal consecrated to *bedek habayis*, not a firstborn donkey.

# v_Leviticus_27 28-29
**Cherem.** **מכל אשר לו**: only part of his property (8:4). "מאדם" means his Canaanite slaves (Rashi). One may not devote what one cannot sell, such as children or Hebrew slaves (8:5). **לא ימכר ולא יגאל**: things devoted for kohanim have no redemption. **כל חרם קדש קדשים הוא** is R' Yehudah ben Beseira's proof that an unspecified cherem goes to *bedek habayis*. The Chachamim use it to show cherem applies even to *kodashim* (8:6–7; Rashi sets out both sides). **כל חרם אשר יחרם מן האדם לא יפדה**: one sentenced to death has no *erech* or *damim* (1:3; Rashi). R' Chanina ben Akavya uses it to exclude *kofer*.

# v_Leviticus_27 30-34
**Ma'aser sheni and ma'aser beheimah.** These complete the parshah but are not part of Arachin. Rashi: only the owner adds a fifth (ממעשרו). The tenth animal is marked with a red-dyed rod. The closing verse, **אלה המצות**, is read in the Sifra as "no prophet may add anything new from now on".

# v_Leviticus_25 8-13
**Yovel.** It is counted as seven *shemittos* and sanctified by beis din, with the shofar blown on Yom Kippur. **וקראתם דרור בארץ לכל יושביה**: the Sifra reads "when all its inhabitants are on it". Yovel therefore lapsed once Reuven, Gad and half of Menashe were exiled. That is when fields are redeemed at value and the owners "open first" (8:1).

# v_Leviticus_25 14-17
**Selling by the years.** **במספר שני תבואת ימכר לך**: a field is priced by the harvests left until Yovel. The Rabbis derive from it that the seller may not redeem within **two** years ("the minimum of 'years' is two"). Barren years do not count, and "three harvests in two years" is possible (9:1; Rashi). **לא תונו** is *ona'as mamon*. Verse 17 is *ona'as devarim* (hurtful words).

# v_Leviticus_25 18-22
**The promise of plenty** for *shemittah*. Rashi notes that the seventy years of the Babylonian exile matched seventy neglected *shemittos*. These verses are included for continuity.

# v_Leviticus_25 23-28
**Redeeming a field.** **והארץ לא תמכר לצמתת כי לי הארץ**. The *go'el* (redeemer) may redeem against the buyer's will. **ואיש כי לא יהיה לו גאל**: "is there anyone in Israel without relatives?" Rashi explains it means none who can afford it. **והשיגה ידו ומצא כדי גאלתו**: not by selling another field, not by borrowing, and not by halves (9:2). **וחשב את שני ממכרו... והשיב את העדף לאיש אשר מכר לו**: the price is prorated, and the calculation favours the seller when the field was resold (9:2). **עד שנת היובל**: it returns at the very start of the Yovel year (Rashi).

# v_Leviticus_25 29-31
**Walled cities and villages.** **בית מושב עיר חומה** means walled since the days of Yehoshua (Sifra; 9:6). It is redeemable at once but only for **a full year** (9:3). After that it is the buyer's permanently, **לצמיתות** (9:4). **אשר לא חומה** is written לא and read **לו** (the *ketiv* is shown in parentheses in the text above). It means "although it has no wall now, it once had one" (Rashi; 9:6). **בתי החצרים... על שדה הארץ יחשב**: village houses have the advantages of both houses and fields (9:7).

# v_Leviticus_25 32-34
**Levite cities.** **גאלת עולם תהיה ללוים**: leviim, and kohanim too, sell and redeem at any time (7:5, 9:8). Rebbi and the Chachamim dispute whether this depends on the person or the city (9:8). **ושדה מגרש עריהם לא ימכר**: "not be changed", so field, *migrash* and city each keep their character (9:8). Rashi also reads it as a gizbar's sale. A Levi's consecrated field does not go to the kohanim at Yovel.

# v_Exodus_21 28-32
**The goring ox** (3:3). **אם כפר יושת עליו ונתן פדיון נפשו**: the *kofer* for a free man is his value. The Mishnah's "נותן את שוויו" follows Bartenura's reading that it is the **victim's** value. **אם עבד יגח השור... כסף שלשים שקלים**: thirty sela'im for any slave, the finest or the poorest.

# v_Exodus_22 15-16
**The mefateh**, seducer (3:4). **כמהר הבתולת**: the Gemara links this to the fifty of Devarim 22:29. Boshes and pegam are paid in addition.

# v_Deuteronomy_22 13-19
**Motzi shem ra** (3:5). **וענשו אתו מאה כסף... כי הוציא שם רע**: the penalty is for the bad name itself, not for exposing her to death (Gemara, cited by Tosafos Yom Tov). Verse 14 reads **והוצא**, written defectively. Some editions have והוציא.

# v_Deuteronomy_22 28-29
**The ones**, rapist (3:4). **ונתן האיש השכב עמה... חמשים כסף**: "for the pleasure of lying". Boshes and pegam are paid on top (Gemara).

# v_Numbers_13 32
**ויציאו דבת הארץ**: the spies' report, the lashon hara for which "the decree on our fathers was sealed" (3:5, Rambam). The Aleppo-based text spells it ויצִיאו without the *vav*.

# v_Numbers_14 22
**וינסו אתי זה עשר פעמים**. The word **זה** shows that *this* sin alone sealed the decree (Rashi, Yachin; 3:5).

# v_Numbers_14 36-37
**The spies' death.** "וימתו האנשים מוציאי דבת הארץ רעה במגפה". Note the *ketiv* (וילונו) and *kri* (וַיַּלִּינוּ) in 14:36.

# v_Numbers_18 14
**כל חרם בישראל לך יהיה**: the Chachamim's view that an unspecified cherem goes to the kohanim (8:6). R' Shimon's reason that kohanim cannot devote, "the *charamim* are theirs", also comes from here (8:5).

# v_Leviticus_1 3
**לרצנו**: "we compel him until he says 'I want'" (5:6). It is the same with a *get* that beis din compels.

# v_Deuteronomy_15 19
**הזכר תקדיש**, against **לא יקדיש איש אתו** (Vayikra 27:26): R' Yishmael's two verses on the *bechor* (8:7).
